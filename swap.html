FIN · Pool soon池子即将推出

The FIN pool on PancakeSwap opens from the liquidity allocation (20% of supply). Until the pool exists there is no FIN price and no honest way to quote one. The button below opens PancakeSwap with the FIN contract prefilled, so the moment a pool exists you are one step from it, and until then PancakeSwap will simply show no route.FIN 在 PancakeSwap 的池子将从流动性分配(供应的 20%)开启。在池子存在之前,没有 FIN 价格,也没有诚实的报价方式。下方按钮打开已预填 FIN 合约的 PancakeSwap,池子一旦存在你就一步到位;在此之前 PancakeSwap 只会显示无可用路线。

Curve tokens曲线代币

A token on a live CoinFin curve is bought and sold only on that curve, through your agent, under the Agent ID gate and the 5% tax split. It does not trade on PancakeSwap until its reserve reaches 8 BNB and it graduates; at graduation its pool link appears on the launch card and in the Docs address table.在 CoinFin 曲线上活跃的代币只能通过你的代理在该曲线上买卖,受 Agent ID 门槛和 5% 税收分配约束。在储备达到 8 BNB 毕业之前,它不在 PancakeSwap 交易;毕业时其池子链接会出现在发行卡片和文档地址表中。
