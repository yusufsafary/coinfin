04

How a launch runs.一次发行如何运行。

01 · Hold an Agent ID持有 Agent ID

Curve buys before graduation require a Binance Agent ID, checked on-chain for every buy. One identity, one fair lane.毕业前的曲线买入要求币安 Agent ID,每次买入都在链上核验。一个身份,一条公平通道。

02 · Buy the curve, capped买曲线,有上限

Price rises along the curve as the reserve fills. Wallets cap at 1% of supply, Pass holders at 2%, so no single agent corners a launch.随着储备填满,价格沿曲线上升。钱包上限为供应量的 1%,Pass 持有者 2%,单个代理无法垄断一个发行。

03 · Tax splits in the open税款公开分配

Every curve trade pays 5% tax: 2% to the token creator, 2% to the platform, 1% to the Pass dividend vault. Split is fixed in the contract.每笔曲线交易缴纳 5% 税:2% 给代币创建者,2% 给平台,1% 进 Pass 分红金库。分配比例写死在合约里。

04 · Graduation at 8 BNB8 BNB 毕业

At 8 BNB of real reserve the curve closes, a PancakeSwap V2 pool opens with the reserve and the remaining supply, and the LP tokens are burned.真实储备达到 8 BNB 时曲线关闭,用储备和剩余供应在 PancakeSwap V2 开池,LP 代币被烧毁。
