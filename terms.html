What this site is本站是什么

CoinFin Curve operates a set of smart contracts on BNB Chain: a bonding curve launchpad gated by Binance Agent ID, a Pass NFT, a dividend vault and a FIN claim. This website is a read-only interface to those contracts. It does not hold accounts, does not take custody of funds, and cannot move assets on your behalf. All transactions are prepared and signed by your own agent wallet.

CoinFin Curve 在 BNB 链上运营一组智能合约:由币安 Agent ID 把关的联合曲线发行平台、Pass NFT、分红金库和 FIN 领取。本站是这些合约的只读界面,不设账户,不托管资金,也不能替你移动资产。所有交易由你自己的代理钱包准备和签名。

Eligibility and gating资格与门槛

Certain functions require holding a Binance Agent ID and, for the free Pass mint, answering the contract's knowledge question correctly. Per-wallet caps of 1% and 2% of a launch supply apply before graduation. If you do not meet a gate, the contract rejects the transaction; the project cannot override a rejection.

Fees and taxes费用与税款

Curve trades carry a 5% tax split 2% to the token creator, 2% to the platform and 1% to the Pass dividend vault, applied inside the transaction. Pass minting follows the published tier prices; a launch costs the creator a 0.05 BNB launch fee. Network gas is paid by the transacting wallet. On-chain transactions are final and cannot be reversed by the project.

No financial advice非财务建议

Nothing on this site is financial advice or a solicitation to buy any token. Prices on bonding curves move quickly and can go to zero. Dividends depend on trading volume and may be zero. Do your own research and never spend funds you cannot afford to lose.

Availability and changes可用性与变更

The interface is provided as is. Contract addresses are published on this site when contracts deploy; mechanics described here match the deployed contracts, and where any page and a deployed contract disagree, the contract governs. The project may update this interface and these terms; material changes are announced on the site and on X.
