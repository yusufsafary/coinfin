How CoinFin works, step by stepCoinFin 如何运作，一步一步

Buying shares购买份额

  • You need a Binance Agentic Wallet agent and a Binance Agent ID in the same wallet. Without an Agent ID, the contract rejects the purchase.你需要一个 Binance Agentic Wallet 代理，并且同一钱包中持有 Binance Agent ID。没有 Agent ID，合约会拒绝购买。
  • Copy the buy prompt from the homepage, or point your agent at the skill file: 复制首页的购买提示，或让你的代理读取技能文件：https://coinfin.fun/coinfin-skill.md
  • Your agent quotes the exact BNB cost (shares times $0.20 in BNB, locked in the contract) and executes only after your confirmation in the Binance app flow.你的代理会报出确切的 BNB 成本（份数乘以合约锁定的 0.20 美元等值），并在你确认后才执行。
  • Each purchase opens a position. Positions from separate purchases harvest independently and are all collected by one claim call.每次购买开启一个仓位。不同购买的仓位独立收获，由一次领取统一收走。

Harvesting收获

  • A position earns one unit per share per full day since purchase. Partial days do not count yet, and unclaimed days never expire.仓位自购买起，每个完整日每份产生一个单位。不足一天的部分暂不计，未领取的天数永不过期。
  • Ask your agent to harvest. It reads your accumulated units, claims them all in one transaction, and FIN lands in your wallet. Claiming costs no fee, only network gas paid by your wallet.让你的代理收获。它会读取你累积的单位，在一笔交易中全部领取，FIN 进入你的钱包。领取不收费，只需钱包支付网络 gas。
  • Rates halve as the network claims. Claiming earlier in an epoch pays the higher rate for every unit inside it.费率随全网领取量减半。在周期内越早领取，该周期内的每个单位都按更高费率支付。

FAQ常见问题

What is the Founder Round?什么是创始人轮？
A presale for the first 5,000 shares. Founder positions harvest 25% more units per day forever, receive 1,000 FIN per share after the round closes, and are marked on the site board. The round split is 70% project and 30% pool; public sale after the round is 50/50. The counter on the homepage is read live from the contract.前 5,000 份份额的预售。创始人仓位每天多收获 25% 单位，永久有效，轮次结束后按每份获得 1,000 FIN，并在网站看板上标记。轮次分配为 70% 项目方、30% 流动池；轮次之后的公开销售为 50/50。首页计数器从合约实时读取。

Can I sell or transfer my shares?我可以出售或转让份额吗？
Positions are bound to the wallet that bought them and cannot be transferred. FIN itself is a normal token and trades freely on PancakeSwap once the pool is live.仓位绑定购买钱包，不可转让。FIN 本身是普通代币，流动池上线后可在 PancakeSwap 自由交易。

What happens before the pool is seeded?流动池注入之前会怎样？
The liquidity half of every payment waits in the contract reserve, visible to everyone through a public getter. The engine starts processing automatically as soon as the pair exists. No reserve funds can be withdrawn by the owner.每笔付款的流动性一半会留在合约储备中，任何人都能通过公开读取函数查看。交易对一旦存在，引擎自动开始处理。所有者无法提取储备资金。

Why does the price move when people buy shares?为什么有人买份额时价格会动？
Because the pool engine spends half of every payment inside the pool: it buys FIN with one quarter of the payment and pairs another quarter as new liquidity. New demand literally walks the price up and deepens the pool at the same time.因为流动池引擎把每笔付款的一半花在池子里：用四分之一购买 FIN，再用四分之一配对为新流动性。新需求实实在在地推高价格，同时加深流动池。

Is this site a trading interface?这个网站是交易界面吗？
No. This site only publishes information, live counters read from the contracts, and the agent skill file. It never asks you to connect a wallet, and buying or claiming happens only through your own agent.不是。本站只发布信息、从合约读取的实时计数以及代理技能文件。它从不要求连接钱包，购买与领取只经由你自己的代理。
