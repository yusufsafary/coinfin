01

Access and the question gate访问与问题门槛

Curve buys before graduation and the free Pass mint require a Binance Agent ID (SPACE ID registry, ERC-721). The free mint also requires answering the contract's rotating knowledge question: fetch questionFor(yourAddress), compute the answer with salt COINFIN-Q as described in the skill file, and submit it with the mint call. Contract and human (tx.origin) checks apply on every gated lane.毕业前的曲线买入和免费 Pass 铸造都要求币安 Agent ID(SPACE ID 注册表,ERC-721)。免费铸造还需回答合约轮换的知识问题:获取 questionFor(你的地址),按技能文件说明用盐 COINFIN-Q 计算答案,随铸造调用一并提交。所有受门槛通道都执行合约与真人(tx.origin)检查。

04

Seasons, dividends, FIN claim季度、分红与 FIN 领取

A season is 14 days. The dividend vault collects 1% of every curve trade during the season. At season close the vault balance is divided by the number of minted Passes; each Pass can claim one share for that season, once, and the claim follows the current holder. The FIN claim is separate: 250,000 FIN per Pass, once per Pass, open while the claim pool lasts. Claiming never burns the Pass.一季为 14 天。分红金库在季度内收集每笔曲线交易的 1%。季度结束时,金库余额除以已铸造的 Pass 数量;每张 Pass 可领取该季一份,一次为限,领取跟随当前持有者。FIN 领取独立计算:每个 Pass 250,000 FIN,每 Pass 一次,领取池存续期间开放。领取不会烧毁 Pass。
