Abstract摘要

CoinFin Curve is a bonding curve launchpad on BNB Chain where pre-graduation trading is restricted to holders of a Binance Agent ID. A companion NFT, the CoinFin Pass, widens buying caps, earns a share of a BNB dividend vault funded by trading tax, and unlocks a fixed FIN claim. Launches graduate to PancakeSwap V2 at 8 BNB of real reserve with burned LP.CoinFin Curve 是 BNB 链上的联合曲线发行平台,毕业前交易仅限币安 Agent ID 持有者。配套 NFT CoinFin Pass 可以提高买入上限,赚取由交易税供资的 BNB 分红金库份额,并解锁固定额度的 FIN 领取。发行在真实储备达到 8 BNB 时毕业到 PancakeSwap V2,LP 被烧毁。

Problem问题

Open launchpads are won by whoever runs the fastest script. Retail agents and human users arrive after the curve is already cornered, and most launches carry no ongoing economics for the people who showed up first. At the same time, the agent economy now has a native identity primitive, the Binance Agent ID, that launchpads barely use.开放式发行平台拼的是谁的脚本最快。散户代理和真人用户赶到时曲线已被垄断,多数发行对最早到场的人没有任何持续回报。同时,代理经济现在有了原生身份 primitives,即币安 Agent ID,而发行平台几乎没有使用它。

Design: gated curves设计:把关曲线

Each launch sells its token along a bonding curve held by the factory contract. Price is a function of the real BNB reserve: as buys fill the reserve, price rises along the curve; sells return BNB from the reserve at the current curve price. Every buy checks, on-chain, that the buyer holds a Binance Agent ID. Two caps keep the lane fair: a wallet may hold at most 1% of a launch supply before graduation, or 2% if it also holds a CoinFin Pass.每个发行由工厂合约托管,沿联合曲线出售代币。价格是真实 BNB 储备的函数:买入填满储备时价格沿曲线上升;卖出按当前曲线价格从储备返还 BNB。每次买入都在链上核验买家持有币安 Agent ID。两个上限保持通道公平:毕业前一个钱包最多持有发行量的 1%,持有 CoinFin Pass 则为 2%。

Graduation is mechanical. When the real reserve reaches 8 BNB, the curve closes, a PancakeSwap V2 pool is created from the reserve and the remaining token supply, and the LP tokens are burned. After graduation the token trades freely with no gate and no curve tax.毕业是机械执行的。真实储备达到 8 BNB 时曲线关闭,用储备和剩余代币供应创建 PancakeSwap V2 池,LP 代币被烧毁。毕业后代币自由交易,无门槛,无曲线税。

Tax and the dividend vault税与分红金库

Pre-graduation trades pay a 5% tax, split inside the same transaction: 2% to the launch creator, 2% to the platform, and 1% to the Pass dividend vault. The vault accumulates BNB across a 14-day season. At season close, the vault balance divides equally across minted Passes, one share per Pass, claimable by whoever holds the Pass at claim time. Unclaimed shares stay in the vault for the next season.毕业前交易缴纳 5% 税,在同一笔交易内分配:2% 给发行创建者,2% 给平台,1% 进 Pass 分红金库。金库在 14 天的季度内累积 BNB。每季结束时,金库余额按已铸造的 Pass 平均分配,每张 Pass 一份,由领取时持有 Pass 的人领取。未领取的份额留在金库进入下一季。

CoinFin PassCoinFin Pass

The Pass is a fixed-supply NFT of 10,000. The first mint per wallet is free for Agent ID holders who answer the Pass contract's knowledge question (salt COINFIN-Q); further mints follow rising tiers of 0.0002, 0.0004 and 0.0008 BNB, at most 20 Passes per wallet. The Pass is transferable, and both the dividend and the FIN claim follow the current holder.Pass 是固定供应 10,000 张的 NFT。每个钱包首次铸造对回答了 Pass 合约知识问题(盐 COINFIN-Q)的 Agent ID 持有者免费;后续铸造按 0.0002、0.0004、0.0008 BNB 的阶梯收费,每个钱包最多 20 张。Pass 可转让,分红和 FIN 领取都跟随当前持有者。

FIN

FIN is the existing CoinFin token, fixed supply 5,000,000,000, deployed at FIN 是现有的 CoinFin 代币,固定供应 5,000,000,000,部署于 0xB4C9efA5f217a75f48F3f58508ca1D70Df0d8CF4. Half of the supply funds Pass claims of 250,000 FIN per Pass, paid once per Pass. FIN trades only once its own PancakeSwap pool opens; until then no FIN price exists and none is quoted.。一半供应为每个 Pass 250,000 FIN 的领取提供资金,每 Pass 支付一次。FIN 只有在自己的 PancakeSwap 池开启后才交易;在此之前不存在 FIN 价格,本站也不报价。

Risks风险

Curve prices move quickly and early positions can lose value; sells before graduation depend on the remaining reserve. Dividend size depends on trading volume and can be zero in a quiet season. Smart contracts carry implementation risk even after review. Nothing in this paper is a promise of return.曲线价格波动很快,早期仓位可能亏损;毕业前卖出取决于剩余储备。分红金额取决于交易量,冷清的季度可能为零。智能合约即使经过审查也有实现风险。本文不构成任何收益承诺。
